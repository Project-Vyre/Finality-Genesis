If Modpack Update Checker has prevented you from launching your world, you can modify the world's `level.dat` file through [**Visual Studio Code**](https://code.visualstudio.com/) with the [**NBT Viewer**](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=Misodee.vscode-nbt) extension by Misode installed *or* through [NBTExplorer](https://github.com/jaquadro/NBTExplorer).

The process of installing VSCode and the NBT Viewer extension should be fairly straightforward. As for NBTExplorer, you have to look for *Releases* and then download the artifact that has the `.msi` file extension and then run NBTExplorer from there.

After opening the world's `world.dat` file, locate **modpackInfo** as you scroll down the **data** list after expanding it. Click the remove button in VSCode then just Ctrl + S to save your modifications.
